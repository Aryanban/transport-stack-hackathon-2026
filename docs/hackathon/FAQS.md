# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

*Extracted directly from the official Where U Elevate challenge drill.*

### Q: Who can participate?
**A:** Students, researchers, startups, and working professionals, in India or abroad, in teams of 2 to 3 members.

### Q: Is there a registration fee?
**A:** No. Participation is completely free.

### Q: Which problem should we solve in Phase 1?
**A:** Pick either **Problem 1** (bus travel-time planner) or **Problem 2** (crowdedness-aware planner). Problems 3, 4, and 5 open to shortlisted teams in Phase 2.

### Q: How do we get the data?
**A:** Sign up on the Delhi Transport Stack platform at [https://delhi.transportstack.in](https://delhi.transportstack.in) and request the datasets your problem needs. Sample datasets and documentation are also packaged in this repository under `/data`.

### Q: What do we submit in Phase 1?
**A:** A proposal, a working prototype, source code with a README, a short technical note, and a short demo video. Mock-only or slide-only submissions do not qualify.

### Q: Is there an on-site round?
**A:** Both Phase 1 and Phase 2 are completely online. Shortlisted teams present in-person at the Transport Stack Annual Event at IIIT Delhi on 11 December 2026.

### Q: How many teams will reach Phase 2?
**A:** About 10–12 teams will be shortlisted.

### Q: What happens in Phase 2?
**A:** Shortlisted teams have about two weeks to refine their Phase 1 work or take on Problem 3, 4, or 5, with a short mentor session every week.

### Q: How will submissions be judged?
**A:** On problem relevance, technical depth and correct use of the data, feasibility, innovation, and usability and impact. Each brief lists its own success criteria.

### Q: Can we use open-source libraries or AI tools?
**A:** Yes, as long as they are credited and the solution is the team's own work.

### Q: Who owns the solutions?
**A:** Teams keep credit for their work. Standout solutions may be taken forward as open-source components on Transport Stack.

### Q: Who do we contact with questions?
**A:** For problems and data, email `transportstack.hackathon@gmail.com` or `csm@iiitd.ac.in`. For registration and platform issues, contact the Where U Elevate helpdesk.

### Q: Do I need to declare my team size during registration?
**A:** Yes. Declare your team size first (2–3 members), and then add your members’ details.
